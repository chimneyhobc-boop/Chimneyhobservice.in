Privacy Policy

Chimney & Hob Care | Last Updated: 7 November 2026

Welcome to Chimney & Hob Care. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website or contact us for service enquiries.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a booking request or contact us, we may receive the following information:

  • Your name
  • Mobile number
  • Service address
  • Requested service type
  • Preferred appointment date
  • Details about your appliance problem

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to customer enquiries.
  • Processing and coordinating service requests.
  • Contacting customers about appointments.
  • Providing information about requested services and charges.
  • Improving our customer service.

3. WhatsApp Communication

Our website may open WhatsApp with your booking details. The message is not shared automatically. You must press the Send button in WhatsApp to send it to us.

WhatsApp communication is also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy and terms.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell customers' personal information. Information may be shared when necessary to provide a requested service, comply with applicable laws or respond to lawful requests.

5. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect customer information. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for service coordination, business records or legal requirements.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party platforms such as WhatsApp. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

7. Your Choices

You may contact us to request information about the personal details you have shared or to raise a privacy-related concern, subject to applicable laws and record-retention requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for general household customers. We do not knowingly request personal information from children without appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
