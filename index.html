Appliance Care

Common Kitchen Appliance Problems

Learn about common problems that may require inspection or maintenance.

Chimney Has Low Suction

Grease accumulation, filter blockage or other mechanical issues may affect airflow and require inspection.

Chimney Making Noise

Unusual noise or vibration may be related to internal components, loose fittings or motor-related concerns.

Chimney Needs Cleaning

Regular cleaning helps remove accumulated oil and grease from accessible filters and surfaces.

Hob Ignition Not Working

Ignition problems may involve the ignition system, electrical supply or other components.

Gas Stove Burner Problems

Burner performance concerns may require professional inspection and appropriate cleaning or maintenance.

Uneven Flame Concerns

Irregular flame patterns should be checked by a qualified service professional before further use.
